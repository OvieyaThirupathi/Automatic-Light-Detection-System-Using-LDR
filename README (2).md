  
**AUTOMATIC LIGHT DETECTION SYSTEM USING LDR**

**1\. Aim**

To design and construct an automatic light detection system using an LDR (Light Dependent Resistor) that automatically switches an LED ON in darkness and switches it OFF when sufficient light is present.

**2\. Problem Statement**

In normal lighting systems, the light has to be switched ON and OFF manually. This can lead to unnecessary power consumption when lights remain ON during daytime.

An automatic system is therefore required to detect the surrounding light level and control the lighting without continuous manual operation.

**3\. Proposed Solution**

The proposed system uses an LDR sensor to detect the intensity of surrounding light.

The resistance of the LDR changes according to the amount of light falling on its surface. This change is used by the circuit to control the LED.

During darkness → LED turns ON

During sufficient light → LED turns OFF

Thus, the system provides automatic lighting based on the surrounding light condition.

**4\. Components Required**

LDR (Light Dependent Resistor)

LED

Resistors

Transistor

Breadboard

**5\. Sensor Working**

The main sensing component in this project is the LDR.

An LDR is a light-sensitive resistor whose resistance changes according to the intensity of light falling on it.

In bright conditions, the resistance of the LDR becomes low.

In dark conditions, the resistance of the LDR becomes high.

This change in resistance is used to control the switching circuit connected to the LED.

**6\. Circuit Working**

The LDR continuously senses the surrounding light level.

When sufficient light falls on the LDR, the circuit keeps the LED OFF.

When the surrounding area becomes dark, the resistance of the LDR changes and the switching circuit activates the LED.

Therefore, the LED automatically responds to changes in the surrounding light without requiring manual switching.

Light detected → LED OFF  
Darkness detected → LED ON

7**. Procedure**

1\. Place the LDR, LED and other components on the breadboard.

2\. Connect the LDR as the light sensing element.

3\. Connect the required resistors and transistor to form the switching circuit.

4\. Connect the LED with correct polarity.

5\. Connect the battery to the circuit.

6\. Check all the connections carefully.

7\. Switch ON the power supply.

8\. Expose the LDR to normal light and observe the LED.

9\. Cover the LDR or reduce the surrounding light.

10\. Observe the LED turning ON automatically.

11\. Expose the LDR to light again and observe the LED turning OFF.

**10\. Result**

The automatic light detection system was successfully constructed and tested using an LDR sensor.

The LED remained OFF when sufficient light was detected. When the LDR was placed in darkness, the LED automatically turned ON.

Thus, the system successfully demonstrated automatic light-based switching.

The system automatically controls an LED according to the surrounding light conditions. It provides a simple understanding of light sensing, resistance variation and automatic switching.
